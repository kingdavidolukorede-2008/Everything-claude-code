Our story

We cook the way your mama would.

Nice Meal was born from a simple belief — that good food shouldn't be expensive, complicated, or hard to find. Right here on Aina Obembe Street in Idimu, we prepare fresh Nigerian meals every day using real ingredients, honest recipes, and a whole lot of care. Our spacious, air-conditioned dining hall with ceiling fans means you always eat in comfort.

From our jollof rice to our egusi, every plate we serve is made with the kind of love that keeps people coming back. We're proud to be Baruwa and Ipaja's go-to spot for meals that feel like home.

— The Nice Meal Family
