What we run

Four arms,
one gate.

Most farms pick one thing. We run four because each one solves a cost problem for the next — which is why our feed bill is 41% smaller than it should be, and why the price you pay stays steady when the market doesn't.

01 — CROPS

Maize, cassava, ugu, plantain

Twelve hectares of rain-fed and irrigated field. Maize and soy go straight to the feed mill; cassava goes to the processing shed; vegetables go to market the morning they're cut.

Takes in
Composted poultry litter · nutrient-rich pond water
Sends out
Grain for feed · residue for fish · fresh produce
Buy from this arm→
02 — POULTRY

8,000 layers, broilers in batches

Deep-litter houses, vaccinated stock, eggs collected twice daily. Broiler batches of 1,500 are staggered so there is dressed chicken leaving the cold room every fortnight.

Takes in
On-farm maize & soy · milled cassava peel
Sends out
Litter for compost · eggs · live & dressed birds
Buy from this arm→
03 — PONDS

Six earthen catfish ponds

Stocked at 40 fingerlings per square metre and harvested on a rolling cycle, so there is table-size catfish available almost every week rather than twice a year.

Takes in
Crop residue · poultry offal in the feed blend
Sends out
Fertile pond water for irrigation · fresh & smoked fish
Buy from this arm→
04 — PROCESSING

Mill, smokehouse, cold room

The hub in the middle. Cassava becomes garri and flour, catfish goes into the smokehouse, birds go into the cold room. Surplus never rots — it changes form and gains shelf life.

Takes in
Whatever the other three cannot sell fresh
Sends out
Garri · cassava flour · smoked fish · frozen chicken
